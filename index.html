About

I'm a final-year Computer Engineering student at Colorado State University, minoring in Computer Science with an Embedded Systems concentration. My work sits at the intersection of hardware and software — I like projects where a control loop, a PCB, and a line of firmware all have to work together correctly, in real time.

Over the past two years I've led the low-voltage electrical architecture for CSU's Formula SAE electric vehicle program, rewritten the real-time motor-control firmware for a 12-motor quadrupedal robot, and supported the university's high-performance computing infrastructure as an HPC developer intern. I'm graduating in May 2026 and applying to graduate programs in Computer Engineering.
